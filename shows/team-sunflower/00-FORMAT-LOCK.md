> ### The runtime range is the one worth closing first
>
> Jane gave 1 to 3 minutes, which is a real answer about the deal and not a real
> answer about the format. A 60 second episode and a 180 second episode are
> structurally different shows: one holds a single turn, the other holds a turn plus
> a reversal. Every rule about where the turn lands, how many beats fit before the
> exit, and what a gate episode can carry is priced against that number.
>
> Drafting against "1 to 3" means drafting against 2 minutes and hoping, which is the
> guessed lock this whole setup exists to prevent.
>
> Do not resolve this by picking the middle. Ask what the delivered episodes actually
> run, or take the runtime of the last approved episode of a comparable title and use
> that. Then write it here as one number and log the change below.
>
> Same for episode count. 50 versus 100 changes where the season question can sit and
> how much runway the opponent web needs.

> ### What this file blocks, and what it does not
>
> Blocked until runtime is one number: drafting episode text, filling the grid,
> placing the gate.
>
> Not blocked, work on these freely: premises, twists, titles, hooks, character work,
> the opponent web, market research, and anything in the show bible.
