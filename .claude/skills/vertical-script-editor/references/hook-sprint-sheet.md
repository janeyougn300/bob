HOOK SPRINT SHEET. Ten hooks per premise. Written in one sitting, scored cold, most of them thrown away. The sprint is the point: hooks one through four are always the ones you already had.

PREMISE: ______________________________________________
DATE: ____________ SCORED BY: ____________

FORM. Each hook is two lines and no more.
LINE 1: the opening image, staged for 9:16, one element moving.
LINE 2: the first spoken line, fifteen words maximum.
No card, no caption, no voiceover, no on-screen text explaining who anyone is. If the hook needs a card to work, it scores zero on VISUAL.

SHAPE QUOTA. Across the ten, produce at least two of each of the following. Tally in the SHAPE column.
W: the first line names a want, a price, or a deadline.
T: somebody gives something up and somebody takes it, on camera.
R: a face already reacting, and the line that caused it arrives second.
S: the audience is handed a fact the lead does not have.

SCORING. Three axes, zero to two each, six available. Score cold, in one pass, without rereading the premise.

IMMEDIATE CONFLICT
0. Something is being established. A person arrives, a place is shown, a mood is set.
1. Conflict exists but is stated rather than happening. Someone refers to a problem.
2. Somebody is losing something inside the two lines, and it is happening now.

OBVIOUS POWER DYNAMIC
0. A stranger cannot tell who outranks whom.
1. The ranking is inferable from costume, room or tone, but nothing enforces it on camera.
2. One person can make the other comply and does so inside the two lines.

VISUAL WITHOUT SETUP TEXT
0. Needs a card, a voiceover, or a previous episode to parse.
1. Parses, but the charge depends on knowing who these people are.
2. A viewer with zero context reads the situation from the image alone. The line adds to it and is not required by it.

THRESHOLD
6: goes on the grid.
5: goes on the grid only if the two-point axis is CONFLICT.
4 or under: dead. Do not repair it. The repair is always the same hook with more words.

If nothing scores 5 or more, the failure is the premise and not the hooks. Take the premise back to the CHARACTER ENGINE and check whether anyone in it can take anything from anyone.
